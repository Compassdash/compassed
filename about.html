About

Why CompassED exists

Compass Dash made revision fun: short lessons, games and XP for Years 7 to 9. CompassED is its serious sibling. It is where the actual schoolwork lives, built by the same team and sharing the same accounts.

What it is

A schoolwork platform for KS3: a student portal for lessons, homework, assignments, quizzes, progress and revision, and a separate teacher portal for setting work, marking and watching how a class is doing. It also carries the school-wide bits: announcements, a calendar and notifications.

What it is not

It is not a replacement for Compass Dash. The games, shop, houses and leaderboards stay on Compass Dash. CompassED only reads progress from it, so nothing there changes.

Inspiration

Borrowed from the best, with our own look

We studied the school platforms teachers already know and took the ideas that genuinely help, without copying anyone's branding.

Courses & assignments

Clear modules, due dates and a submissions view, in the spirit of Canvas.

Simplicity

A stream of what matters today and a few taps to hand in work, like Google Classroom.

Lessons & quizzes

Block-based lessons and auto-marked quizzes with explanations, as Moodle does well.

Personalised learning

Mastery checkpoints and a "what next" suggestion, in the spirit of Brightspace.

School communication

Targeted announcements with read receipts, a calendar and notifications, like itslearning.

School integration

Microsoft sign-in, class rosters, reminders and grade sync with Teams for Education.

Privacy & safety

Built for a school

Minimal data

Students are known by a 4-digit code and a username, just like Compass Dash. No email address or real name is needed unless a school links Microsoft accounts.

Separate permissions

Students can only see their own class's work and their own progress. Teachers see only their subject's classes. The database checks this on every request, not just the page.

Nothing public

Submissions, grades, feedback and announcements are only visible to the student concerned and their teachers.

Secure by default

Every page sets a strict content security policy, teacher sessions lock after 20 minutes without use, and sign-in attempts are rate-limited by the same guard Compass Dash uses.
