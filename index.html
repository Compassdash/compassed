For students

Learning that tells you what to do next

No more guessing. CompassED looks at what's due, what you've mastered and what you're forgetting, and puts the one most useful thing at the top of your dashboard.

"What to do next" card

One suggested task, chosen from your deadlines, playlists and review queue. Do it, then the next one appears.

Lessons & playlists

Short lessons with key points and quick checks, lined up by your teacher in the order to work through them.

Mastery checkpoints

Short tests at the end of each unit. Pass the bar and the unit is yours; miss it and you get told exactly what to revisit.

Review queue

Anything you get wrong comes back as a flashcard, spaced out so it moves into long-term memory with a few minutes a day.

Draft, then hand in

Save drafts as you go. Every version is kept, so nothing is lost and teachers can see how your work developed.

One calendar

Deadlines, exams, trips and events together, with one tap to add them to your phone's calendar.

For teachers

A separate portal, built for the job

Switch to the teacher portal and the whole interface changes: your classes, your content, your marking. Students never see it.

Class overview heatmap

Every student against every unit on one screen. Spot the gaps in seconds, including Compass Dash mastery.

Rubric builder

Build a marking grid once, attach it to any assignment and mark by clicking the level each piece of work reaches.

Targeted announcements

Send to a class, a year group or named students, and see who has read it.

Assignments & quizzes

Homework, longer assignments and auto-marked quizzes, with submissions, versions and feedback in one view.

Lesson & playlist editor

Write lessons from simple blocks, then line them up into a playlist for a class.

Microsoft Teams

Sign in with the school account, import class rosters, post reminders to Teams and sync assignments and grades.

Compass Dash connection

Two platforms, one student

Same account

Students sign in with the 4-digit code and username they already use on Compass Dash. Teachers use their Compass Dash teacher account. No new passwords to remember.

Progress flows one way

CompassED reads XP, topic mastery and activity from Compass Dash and shows it beside schoolwork. It never changes anything on Compass Dash, so the games and leaderboards carry on exactly as they are.

Getting started

Three steps

Sign in

Students: your Compass Dash code and username. Teachers: your Compass Dash teacher login. Schools on Microsoft 365 can switch on "Sign in with Microsoft".

Teachers set the work

Create units, lessons and quizzes, line them into playlists, and set homework with a due date and an optional rubric.

Students follow the compass

The dashboard shows what to do next. Work gets handed in, marked and returned with feedback, and progress shows on the heatmap.
